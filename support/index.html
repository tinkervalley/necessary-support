How can we help?

If you're experiencing an issue, have a question, or would like to provide feedback about Necessary, please get in touch.

Contact

Email: support@tinkervalley.ca

When reporting an issue, it helps to include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Account & Data

You can manage your account from within Necessary. For additional help with your account, privacy, or data, contact us using the email address above.

Account Deletion

If you have an account, you can request deletion of your account and associated data from within the Necessary app. If you have trouble completing that process, contact support for assistance.
