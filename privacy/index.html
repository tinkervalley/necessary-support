N Necessary

Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

Overview

Necessary is a household spending app. It uses the information you provide to create your account, sync your household, show your spending, and deliver the features you request. Necessary does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Information Necessary processes

Account information includes your email address and account identifier. Household data can include members, invitations, pay periods, category settings, merchant rules, budget additions, transaction dates, amounts, merchants, account names, review decisions, and the member associated with a transaction.

A device token is stored when you enable push notifications. An import token and transactions waiting to upload can be stored securely on your device when you use Shortcuts or the Transaction Import API.

How information is used

This information is used to authenticate you, sync data across devices, share household information with household members, calculate spending summaries, process transaction imports, prevent duplicate imports, and send requested transaction-review notifications.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database, and server functions. Logo.dev receives a merchant name or website domain when Necessary requests an optional merchant logo. Apple provides platform services such as push notifications, widgets, Shortcuts, and App Store distribution. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing within a household

Members of the same household can view the household’s transactions and settings, including the member associated with a transaction. Invite only people you trust.

Retention and deletion

Information remains available while your account or household requires it. You can delete individual transactions in Necessary. You can permanently delete your account from Settings.

If you are the only household member, account deletion also deletes the household and its data. If other members remain, household ownership transfers to a remaining member and your membership is removed.

Security

Necessary uses Apple Keychain for sensitive tokens on your device and encrypted HTTPS connections for network requests. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Necessary is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Necessary changes. The effective date will be updated when that happens. For privacy or support questions, use the Support link on Necessary’s App Store page.
