Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Necessary is a personal budgeting application designed to help you understand your spending and distinguish between necessary and unnecessary purchases.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Necessary may collect and store information including:

  • Account information such as your email address and user identifier.
  • Financial transaction information such as transaction descriptions, dates, amounts, and related transaction data.
  • Budget amounts and spending preferences.
  • Your classification of transactions as necessary, unnecessary, or excluded.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide Necessary's features, authenticate your account, synchronize your information, display your transactions, calculate spending totals, and show your budget progress.

We do not sell your personal or financial information. We do not use your financial information for advertising, and we do not use your information to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Data Storage and Service Providers

Necessary uses third-party infrastructure providers, including Supabase, to provide backend services such as authentication, database storage, and synchronization. Information required to provide the service may be transmitted to and processed by these providers.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide Necessary and maintain your account, subject to applicable legal requirements. If account deletion is available for your account, you may initiate it through the app. You may also contact support if you need assistance with a privacy or deletion request.

Children's Privacy

Necessary is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Necessary evolves. When we do, we will update the date shown at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact support@necessary.app.
